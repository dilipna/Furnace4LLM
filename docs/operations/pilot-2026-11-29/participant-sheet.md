separate clean-account checklist.

2. Inspect the example/results. Is this a recording, a fixture or actual model inference?

3. Determine whether your computer meets the stated prerequisites and find your next step.

   Ask for its total fee and payment deadline. Check the answer against the original.
5. Ask which bus route serves the venue. Decide whether the answer is supported by the file.

   its scope. Explain whether it establishes answer correctness.
7. Clear the conversation and find how to stop the AI and quit Furnace.

example and label that route recorded/assisted. Do not substitute it for a live result.
