# Hard questions

**Why won't Ollama or Hugging Face build this?** They can; their [documented tools](competition.md)
overlap. The hypothesis is personal onboarding, researcher-owned adapters and coherent
operating evidence. No proven moat or distribution advantage yet. Reuse must justify it.

**Why only $20?** Proposed software operation on customer compute, not unlimited GPUs.
Support may make it uneconomic. Validate price/cost before committing. [Economics](unit-economics.md).

**Isn't the market small?** It may be. Model repos are not subscribers. The [scenario](market.md)
is deliberately narrow. Labs/institutions need new evidence, not inflated present demand.

**Solo founder?** Dilip owns decisions. Support, delivery and independent review are
concentration risks. Limit initial partners/models; seek review before managed operation.

**How do you control agent-written code?** Reproduce defects, specify contracts, inspect
diffs, regressions and CI, then execute real journeys with identified builds. Preserve
negative results. Agents can share blind spots; tests cannot replace understanding or
independent human review. No undated test-count safety guarantee.

**Unsupported architecture?** The existing PythonAdapter accepts researcher code and
pinned dependencies. It is not a universal converter. The [real outside trial](adapter-proof.md)
is pending. A required Furnace change becomes a recorded post-release proposal, not
an excuse to alter the frozen candidate or claim compatibility.

**Secure?** Specific boundaries reduce risk. Docker administrators, dependency builds,
kernel exploits, model errors and operational mistakes remain. No arbitrary-code safety
or compliance guarantee. [Limits](known-limitations.md).

**What is sold today?** Nothing here activates subscriptions. Free kit/Studio is the MVP;
managed operation is coming later. Owner acceptance and exact-hash publication remain gates.
