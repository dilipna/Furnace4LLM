# Bottom-up market — hypothesis, not a valuation

Reviewed November 27, 2026. No top-down AI market figure.

| Countable population | Primary source | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More than 2 million public model repositories, reported for 2025 | [Hugging Face team](https://huggingface.co/blog/huggingface/state-of-os-hf-spring-2026) | Includes derivatives, not unique researchers, active projects or buyers. |
| 5,290 accepted main-track papers from 21,575 valid submissions in 2025 | [NeurIPS program chairs](https://blog.neurips.cc/2025/09/30/reflections-on-the-2025-review-process-from-the-program-committee-chairs/) | Papers, not labs or people. Submissions are not an additive market. |

Do not sum these populations: people and projects overlap. Do not multiply all Hub
accounts by the price. These dated counts are context, not a current measured SAM.

## Worked annual-cohort scenario — assumptions, not forecast

5,290 accepted papers × assumed 10% service-relevant projects × assumed 50% after
deduplicating overlapping authors/projects and unsuitable buyers = 264.5 project
equivalents. Fractional expectations are not an actual prospect list. Assume 5%
become subscribers: 13.225 expected accounts. At proposed $20/month, annualized revenue
would be $3,174 before costs. [Registered inputs/arithmetic](claims.json).

This deliberately narrow scenario shows researcher subscriptions alone may be a small
business. Conversion is unmeasured; team seats and institutions need separate evidence,
procurement and economics. No team price is set. It is not a global TAM or forecast.

## Replace assumptions with an actual prospect count

After release, select a dated cohort of model-releasing papers and linked repositories.
Manually review distinct maintainers/labs, licences, hardware fit, running checkpoint
and existing serving workflow. Record exclusions and overlaps; do not scrape a mass
contact list. With owner approval, invite appropriate maintainers. Measure serving
need, willingness to use, willingness to pay and retained usage separately. Revise
the funnel with consented records. Missing: deduplicated buyers, conversion, budgets
and churn. The business can fail even if the engineering works.
