# Expansion — hypotheses in sequence

Individuals: free inspection/kit and Studio, assisted first deployment. Earn reuse
before selling operation. Measure ongoing support, not just first-run success.

Labs/teams: proposed per-seat collaboration around evaluation identity, shared
responsibility and repeatable environments. Discover machine ownership, approval,
record-sharing and budget needs. Shared accounts, role permissions, centralized
storage and team billing are not present features. No seat price is set.

Universities/companies: proposed private-model operation with audit trails, retention
and procurement/security review. Local records are engineering evidence, not
compliance, tamper-proof logs or institutional readiness. Longer sales and support
need separate economics; no such customers are claimed.

The [document workload](evidence/installed-document.json) demonstrates infrastructure
running an end-user application. It does not prove demand or solve injection.
The researcher's model remains the product's front door.
