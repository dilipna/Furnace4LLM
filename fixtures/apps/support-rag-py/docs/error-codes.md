# Controller error codes

## What does error E14 mean?

E14 means the thermocouple is disconnected; reseat the thermocouple connector and restart the firing.

## What does error E22 mean?

E22 means the kiln exceeded its configured maximum temperature and the firing was stopped automatically.

## What does error E31 mean?

E31 means the controller lost Wi-Fi for more than 10 minutes; the firing continues locally.
